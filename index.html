Latin Then and Now

By Staff Writer: Elliot Keske · Digital Editor: Alex Quinones · November 19, 2026

The Latin of yesteryear in many ways is unrecognizable. Building architecture, cars, and the very classrooms we learn in. I traced Latin’s history through the Latin Yearbook Archive sifting through countless images starting from 1953, all the way to 2000. I also gained special access to photos of Latin through The Chicago History Museum Archives, to which I owe many thanks to the Department of Rights and Reciprocity at the Chicago History Museum, as well as Web Editor Alex Q. for this layout.
